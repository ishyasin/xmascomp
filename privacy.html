Privacy notice

The Pharmacy Christmas Competition 2026 is run by ED&G™ (Evolve Digital & Governance). This notice explains what we collect when you enter and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Your name. Shown on the public leaderboard.
  • Your @nhs.net email address. Used only to make sure each person enters once. Only @nhs.net addresses can enter.
  • Your scores and quiz answers. Used to work out your total and your place on the leaderboard.

We do not use cookies. Your browser keeps a note of your place in the game (session storage) until you close the tab.

Your email address

Your email address is deleted 7 days after the competition has ended and the prizes have been revealed. It is never shown on the leaderboard and never used for anything else.

How long we keep the rest

Your name, scores and quiz answers are kept until 7 days after the competition has closed, then deleted.

Sharing

Your information is not shared with third parties.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your entry by contacting the competition organiser. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
